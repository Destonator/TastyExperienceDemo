We believe food should create memories and bring people together.

Worcester knows us for a creative brunch. In the evening the same chef-driven kitchen turns to dinner, with an atmosphere that is warm, handcrafted and elevated.

We cook with fresh ingredients and house-made sauces, drawing on Brazilian and contemporary flavors: avocado creations, picanha steak, vibrant bowls, house-made sangrias and signature mimosas. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and keto choices share the same menu, so everyone at the table can eat well together.

Our specialty drinks are finished with edible 18k gold dust, non-toxic and fully food-safe.

Marcela Andrade, executive chef
